## Central Limit Theorem

If you were to take a large number of random samples from a population and plot the mean of each sample, the resulting graph (the distribution of sample means) would look like a normal distribution.

## Standard Error (SE)

The SE gives us an indication of how close our sample mean is likely to be to the true population mean. It is the standard deviation of the sampling distribution of sample means.

## Confidence Intervals

A 95% confidence interval tells us that 95% of random samples will contain the true population parameter within their intervals.
